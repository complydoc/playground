National Insurance numberUK high 1A direct identifier under UK data protection law. Uniquely identifies an individual and is routinely treated as high risk in a breach assessment.

Payment card numberinternational high 1Payment card data. Validated with the Luhn checksum and the issuing network's prefix and length. Storing this in unencrypted documents is a PCI DSS problem as well as a data protection one.
